Comic Book

BORROWED TIME

A short graphic narrative about what it really costs to rewind time.

Project Type
Comic Book
Project Duration
4 Months
Tools
Procreate, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign
Team
Solo
Summary

Some moments are meant to pass, not repeat

Borrowed Time is a short graphic narrative that explores the emotional consequences of controlling time. The story follows a young girl who discovers a mysterious watch that allows her to rewind moments, initially using it to fix everyday mistakes and protect her loved ones. However, as she continues to manipulate time, she begins to realize that every action comes with an unseen cost.

Blending themes of sacrifice, grief, and inevitability, the narrative unfolds into a haunting realization that time cannot be controlled without consequence. Through a monochromatic visual style contrasted with symbolic highlights, the comic reflects on the idea that some moments are meant to pass, not repeat.
