Project Overview

Every negative encounter leaves a permanent crack

Shatter is a 2D narrative RPG where a mask, not a health bar, shows emotional strain. Every negative encounter adds a permanent crack, building to a moment where the mask shatters and the character's faith in a supportive society breaks. Made in a game jam to explore social themes through simple mechanics and symbolism.

The Concept

A mask as a metaphor for belief

Shatter explores the fragile belief that society is fair and supportive. The player wears a symbolic mask that cracks with each negative encounter: judgement, hypocrisy, or indifference. The cracks build into a visual record of the character's weakening trust, until the mask shatters and the illusion of a just, caring society collapses.

Finding the Core Idea

  • Exploring themes of societal pressure and emotional vulnerability
  • Using a mask as a metaphor for belief in a fair society
  • Showing how repeated experiences shape emotional perception

Building the Game System

  • Designing exploration and dialogue-based interactions
  • Creating the mask crack system as emotional feedback
  • Replacing traditional health or combat mechanics with symbolism

Refining Through Play

  • Playtesting to improve narrative flow
  • Adjusting player choices and interactions
  • Refining the mask feedback system
