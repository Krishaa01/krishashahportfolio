Project Overview

A journey never finished, walked once more

A multisensory VR experience inspired by a haunting detail from John Marshall's excavation records — a skeleton found beside a broken pot in Mohenjo-daro. The player lives through the final moments of the city's last inhabitant, then returns as a silent witness. In the final act, they retrace the exact footsteps and position of that last person, completing the journey that was never finished.

The Concept

A city dismantled a second time

There is a specific historical fact that stayed with us. When British-era construction workers were laying railway tracks through the Indus Valley in the 1850s, they found an unusual abundance of ready-made bricks in the ground — fired, uniform, ancient. They used them as ballast for the tracks. Those bricks were Harappa Civilization.

An entire layer of a 4000 year old civilization was pulled out of the earth, loaded onto trolleys, and used for railway construction. The workers carried it without knowing what they were carrying. The city was dismantled a second time — quietly, practically, without ceremony.

Concept One · The Mapped World

A VR environment built in Unity where the virtual space is aligned precisely to the real physical space. The sunlight and shadow of the real corridor are mapped to the day and night scenes of the VR world. A cooler unit positioned outside the mapped area pushes cold air into the shadow zone — this becomes the physical sensation of the scene transition from day to night.

Concept Two · The Trolley

A physical trolley with a rotary encoder embedded inside. As the player pushes it forward, the encoder sends positional input to TouchDesigner, which controls a video narrative — the story of the laborer, the bricks, the city. The speed of the push controls the pace of the story. Stop, and time stops. Push back, and the narrative offers a different answer.

Player Journey

Three acts, one unfinished walk

01
The Labour
They push a trolley through a buried corridor. Sand lifts. Walls rebuild. The city resurrects around them with every step forward.
02
The Last Person
The trolley stops. They find a clay pot — the only object in the space — with handprints already on it. They carry it toward a well. Footprints appear ahead, stopping mid-stride, two steps before the water's edge. The player walks those last two steps and places the pot.
03
The Witness
Night. The well is dry. The waterline stain shows where the water once was. Sand returns slowly. Only the pot remains.

Walkthrough

Experience Storyboard

Step 01
Entry

You enter a space of sand and broken walls, with a trolley waiting in front of you. Like a labourer in the 1850s pushing bricks without knowing they were Mohenjo-daro, you push it forward. Your movement controls time, and history unfolds.

Step 02
The Rebuild

As you push, sand falls away and walls rise around you. The civilization returns, and the space reveals the history, memory, and loss carried in the trolley's weight.

Step 03
The Pot

In the rebuilt corridor, sunlight falls on a lone clay pot beside a broken wall. Its warmth draws you in and lets you feel the atmosphere of a living civilization.

Step 04
The Handprints

As you reach out, you find handprints already pressed into the clay. They are silent traces of people who once lived here, and perhaps died here. Someone held this before you.

Step 05
The Footprints

When you lift the pot, animated footprints appear and lead toward the well. It's a trail already walked, inviting you to follow and uncover what happened.

Step 06
The Trail

You follow the long trail as the well comes into view, full of water and reflecting light. The path suggests something important once happened here.

Step 07
The Unfinished Path

Two steps before the well's edge, the footprints stop mid-stride. The journey was never completed, hinting that something happened to the person who made it.

Step 08
The Prompt

You follow the last footprints toward the well. A UI prompt highlights the pot, showing that you must now use it to continue the act.

Step 09
The Dry Well

As you place the pot down, cold wind rises and the scene turns to night. The well is completely dry, with only a waterline stain on its walls, revealing the loss of water, life, and the civilization itself.

Step 10
The Return

Sand slowly returns and the footprints disappear, leaving only the pot where you placed it. You're back where you began, but the bricks are no longer just objects. You now understand the history and loss they carry.
