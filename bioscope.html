Project Overview

A street-side bioscope, reimagined

Looking Through the Lenses is an interactive installation that reimagines the traditional street-side bioscope for a contemporary setting. Created for the Dumas Art Festival, the project takes the form of a handcrafted wooden bioscope designed for six viewers, inspired by the nostalgic experience of looking through small lenses to watch moving images. Inside, four looping surreal video artworks explore imaginative and speculative themes through cinematic storytelling and AI-generated visuals. Each viewer can independently switch between the videos using rotary regulators, making the experience personal and interactive.

The Concept

Nostalgia meets contemporary technology

Looking Through the Lenses combines the nostalgia of the traditional street-side bioscope with contemporary technology. Its handcrafted wooden structure and individual viewing lenses preserve the curiosity and wonder of the original experience, while AI-generated surreal videos introduce contemporary storytelling. Rotary regulators allow viewers to switch between four video artworks, adding a simple layer of interaction. By blending traditional craftsmanship, digital media, and interaction, the project reimagines the bioscope as a contemporary storytelling experience.

Project Glimpses

Inside the Bioscope

Chai na Milegi Dubara

Of the four videos presented inside the bioscope, this piece takes a surreal approach to reimagining an everyday reality. Through AI-generated visuals, cinematic sequences, and speculative storytelling, it creates a world that feels familiar yet strangely out of place — a speculative film imagining a world where tea becomes extinct in Gujarat.

The Concept

When something ordinary suddenly vanishes

This video explores a surreal speculative scenario — a world where tea suddenly becomes extinct. In a place like Gujarat, where tea is deeply woven into everyday life and social culture, its disappearance creates a strange and unsettling reality. The film imagines how people who depend on tea as a daily ritual respond when something so ordinary suddenly vanishes. The visuals were first manually filmed using staged scenes and props based on a scripted narrative, and later transformed using AI tools to create surreal imagery. This process blends real footage with AI manipulation to build a dreamlike interpretation of a familiar cultural habit. The video is one of the four looping narratives presented inside the bioscope installation — viewers can switch to this film using the rotary regulators.
